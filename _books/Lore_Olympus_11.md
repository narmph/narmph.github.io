I actually read this as a webtoon so I'll just write one review for the whole series. The art style feels very textured and is a visual treat (lot's of bold colors and high contrast compositions). The writing is fast passed and gripping but the world building is lacking that something that makes everything feel fully lived in. Where this series looses me is the pacing; the narrative feels a little drawn out until we're suddenly racing through the story and it's abruptly over. I guess that is almost inevitable with a long running webtoon.
